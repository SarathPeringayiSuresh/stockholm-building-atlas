    A WALK WITH A LITTLE MORE TO IT

    Stockholm, on foot.

    Choose a mood, a walking-time target and 2–8 stops. We connect outdoor places using a snapshot of Stockholm’s public streets and footpaths. Round trips return to your starting point.

    Where the places come from

    Building landmarks and recorded years come from the existing Hus för hus atlas. Parks, gardens and viewpoints come from OpenStreetMap contributors. Nature categories are informed by Visit Stockholm. Each stop links to its source.

    Before you head out

    Time is estimated at 4.5 km/h and excludes breaks and visits inside buildings. Stops are outdoor viewing points or park approaches. Routes may include stairs and unpaved paths. This is not an accessibility planner; live closures, opening hours and weather are not included.
